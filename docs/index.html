Everything you need

A full control plane, not just a screen cast.

Mirror, control, capture, and record — from a minimal window OBS can grab, with per-device settings that persist.

Instant USB mirror

Plug in, click Start Mirror, and your screen is live in a dedicated window — no setup, no accounts.

Built for low latency

Present-on-arrival, drop-to-latest, and a frame-latency-1 swapchain so lag can never accumulate.

Full control

Mouse taps & swipes, wheel scroll, right-click Back, and real keyboard input — type straight into the phone.

Device audio

Hear your phone through your PC in sync with the video, with a one-key mute toggle.

Record & snapshot

Ctrl+R records MP4 with audio; Ctrl+S grabs a full-resolution PNG — or trigger both from the dashboard.

Two-way clipboard

Copy on the phone and paste on the PC — and Ctrl+V the other way. Text just flows both directions.

Multiple devices

Mirror several phones at once, each in its own window with independent capture controls.

Auto-reconnect

Unplug, sleep, or wander out of range — the window stays put and resumes the moment it's back.

Quality presets

Balanced, Crisp, or Low-latency — tune resolution, bitrate, and fps per device. Your choices are remembered.

Under the hood

Engineered for the shortest path to your screen.

No FFmpeg, no jitter buffer, no browser in the middle. Just a tight native pipeline from the phone's encoder to your monitor.

Media Foundation · NVDEC

Hardware decode

H.264/H.265 is decoded on the GPU straight to NV12 textures that never leave VRAM — one copy, then a BT.709 shader.

D3D11 · flip-model

Zero-lag presentation

A flip-model waitable swapchain with maximum frame latency of 1, pinned to your NVIDIA GPU and VRR-aware.

scrcpy · adb reverse

Direct USB transport

A scrcpy-compatible reverse tunnel streams frames over USB with no jitter buffer, so nothing sits between capture and decode.
